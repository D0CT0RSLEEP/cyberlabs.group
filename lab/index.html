Rules of engagement

Blue team only. No exploitation, no pwn, no attack playbooks. Curiosity stops at containment.

About

What MOD-HEAVY is

A productized defensive lab experience under CyberLabs. You play a shift analyst: something odd hits a simulated network, and your job is to understand it and stop the bleeding — not to break in.

Related aesthetic language to other CyberLabs lore projects, but this floor is professional ops-console — not arcade CRT. Evidence is fabricated (fake company, documentation-range IPs, hashes labeled SIMULATION).
